Objectives

Why the PolyQ Database exists and what it offers.

What is this database for?
  • PolyQ Database is a means to aid the understanding of polyglutamine diseases.
  • Like other databases, PolyQ focuses on key information and tries to convey it as clearly as possible.
  • It is a tool for anyone with a scientific background to view the most important information on polyglutamine diseases, all in one place.

Why was this made?

Information about polyglutamine disorders is scattered across the internet. A single website with the most important information on every polyglutamine disease is helpful for the scientific community.

What makes this database different from others?

At the time of its creation, no other polyglutamine or rare disease database included general information about every polyglutamine disease: some focus on genetics, others on therapy. Using trusted online sources and the literature, PolyQ consolidates the most searched information on polyglutamine disorders in one website.
