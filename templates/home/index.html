Disease groups

Huntington's disease

Huntington's disease is the most widely studied and best known polyglutamine disease. It is the most common inherited neurodegenerative disease and is mainly characterized by excessive, uncontrolled motor movements.

Dentatorubral-pallidoluysian atrophy

DRPLA is sometimes included with the autosomal dominant cerebellar ataxias type 1, because many of its clinical combinations are also seen in ADCA I diseases. It is found mainly in Japan.

Spinal-bulbar muscular atrophy

Also known as Kennedy disease, SBMA is a genetic disorder with loss of motor neurons. It is inherited in an X-linked manner, so carrier females have a 50% chance of transmitting the mutation to each child.

ADCA I

Autosomal dominant cerebellar ataxias type 1 combine cerebellar ataxia with variable pyramidal, extrapyramidal and neuropathic signs. Four polyglutamine diseases belong here: SCA1, SCA2, SCA3 and SCA17.

ADCA II

Autosomal dominant cerebellar ataxias type 2 present with cerebellar ataxia and retinal degeneration. SCA7 belongs to this group.

ADCA III

Autosomal dominant cerebellar ataxias type 3 manifest pure cerebellar ataxia. Many spinocerebellar ataxias belong here, but SCA6 is the only polyglutamine disease in the group.

About this database
